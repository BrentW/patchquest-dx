The reference recreates the original North American NES Dragon Warrior font. The `-2` in its page address is not a reference to Dragon Warrior II.

Quest Pixel used this recreation as a visual reference. Its AI-assisted project assets contain newly entered bitmap grids, revised proportions and placement, a descending j, footed l, slashed zero, additional character coverage and reusable spacing profiles. Some shapes remain identical after repositioning or closely similar; newly entered data is not a claim that every letterform was independently invented.
